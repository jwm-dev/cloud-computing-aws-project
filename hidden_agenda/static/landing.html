Paper synopsis

A cooperative gridworld where one hidden impostor can stall the mission.

Hidden Agenda is a multi-agent environment built around a sparse coordination loop: collect fuel, reach the deposit, and vote when sabotage is suspected. Observations are intentionally local and role-blind, so the interesting behavior comes from inference, timing, and social pressure rather than privileged state access.

What the app does

This site is the operational surface for the simulation. You can launch batches of games, inspect the run list, and open a replay viewer that scrubs through each stored game step by step.

Three pages, three jobs
  • Landing page for the synopsis and the workflow.
  • Dashboard for one-off or batched dispatches.
  • Viewer for replaying a specific run with a timeline.

Mechanics in brief

Situation phase: agents move, interact with fuel or the deposit, and the impostor can freeze crewmates in a short forward beam.

Voting phase: everyone is moved to the voting room and votes are recorded with a one-step delay in visibility.

Observations: every player gets the same shape of local RGB view, inventory ratio, global progress, and vote matrix.

Replay: every run is logged as deterministic step data, which lets the backend reconstruct frames for the viewer.
